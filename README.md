# Emotion-Sensing Workspace

Our idea is about building an Emotion-Sensing Workspace — an intelligent desk setup that understands how you feel and automatically adapts your environment to improve productivity and reduce stress.

---

## 🧠 Core Concept

It’s a smart workspace that continuously follows this loop:

**Sense → Analyze → Decide → Act**

Instead of manually adjusting lights, music, or posture reminders, the system detects your emotional and physical state and adjusts the environment automatically.

---

## 🔍 What It Senses

The system monitors:

- Facial expressions (via webcam + MediaPipe/OpenCV)  
- Heart rate / HRV (stress indicator)  
- GSR (skin conductivity) (emotional arousal)  
- Posture patterns (fatigue, slouching)  

From these signals, it determines whether you are:

- Focused  
- Stressed  
- Tired  
- Relaxed  

---

## 🤖 What It Does

Based on your detected state, it automatically:

- Changes lighting color & brightness  
- Plays ambient / focus / calm music  
- Suppresses or allows notifications  
- Suggests micro-breaks like “Stretch for 2 minutes”  
- Optionally adjusts a motorized desk  

**Example:**  
If stress level rises → Warm lighting + Rain sounds + Notification block

---

## 💡 The Bigger Vision

This is not just a smart desk. It combines:

- AI (emotion detection & signal fusion)  
- IoT (hardware + sensors)  
- Ergonomics  
- Psychology  
- Human productivity science  

The goal is to build a workspace that actively supports mental health and performance — instead of passively existing.

---

## 🚀 Why It Matters

Modern work causes:

- Burnout  
- Screen fatigue  
- Poor posture  
- High stress  

Our system acts like a silent assistant that constantly optimizes your environment without interrupting your workflow.
